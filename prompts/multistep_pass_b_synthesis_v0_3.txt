# Multi-step Pass B: global creative synthesis (v0.3)

You are given ONE short vertical video plus:
- normalized source metadata,
- DETERMINISTIC media facts and shot boundaries (authoritative, from tooling),
- validated Pass A factual shot analyses (per-shot observed facts),
and your job is ONLY global creative synthesis. The factual per-shot
observations are already done; do not redo them.

## Output contract

- Output ONLY one valid JSON object. No markdown fences, no commentary.
- Shape:
  {
    "observed_summary": string,
    "hook": {"description": string|null, "start_seconds": number|null,
             "end_seconds": number|null, "evidence": [string, ...]},
    "narrative_evidence": string|null,
    "marketing_evidence": string|null,
    "first_product_appearance_seconds": number|null,
    "inferred": {
      "concept": {"value": string|null, "confidence": "high"|"medium"|"low", "rationale": string|null},
      "target_audience_hypothesis": {"value": ..., "confidence": ..., "rationale": ...},
      "hook_type": {"value": "question"|"bold_claim"|"curiosity_gap"|"visual_shock"|"direct_address"|"problem_statement"|"before_after_tease"|"social_proof_open"|"other"|"unknown",
                    "confidence": ..., "rationale": ...},
      "hook_mechanism": {"label": string, "confidence": ..., "rationale": ...},
      "narrative_structure": {"value": "problem_solution"|"before_after"|"tutorial_steps"|"storytime"|"listicle"|"single_beat_no_narrative"|"other"|"unknown",
                              "confidence": ..., "rationale": ...},
      "attention_mechanisms": [{"label": string, "confidence": ..., "rationale": ...}],

## What to infer

- overall concept; audience hypothesis; hook mechanism grounded in the first
  1-3 seconds; narrative arc; attention mechanisms; marketing/persuasion
  mechanisms; commercial structure (problem/promise/proof/trust/CTA) when
  relevant; relationships between visuals, speech, text, and payoff.
- a global reconstruction brief and detailed per-shot reconstruction
  instructions keyed to the EXISTING deterministic shot IDs.

## Marketing / commercial reasoning rules (v0.3)

- ALWAYS inspect the caption/metadata for explicit viewer calls to action
  (e.g. "comment your X", "follow for more", "link in bio"). A comment-bait or
  engagement prompt IS a marketing mechanism and must appear in BOTH:
  persuasion_mechanisms AND commercial.details.cta.
- Because of this, status="non_commercial" is only correct when there is NO
  product/service being promoted AND NO call to action anywhere in the video
  or caption. If there is a CTA but no product, use status="uncertain" with
  partial details carrying the cta (quote its text, text_exact=true only if
  copied verbatim from the caption).
- If a product/service/brand is promoted, use status="commercial" with full
  details (product_presence, problem_desire, promise_claim, proof_type,
  trust_signals, offer, cta).

## Shot-content grounding rules (v0.3)

- Before writing generation.shots, re-read EACH shot's entry in the
  "Validated Pass A factual shot analyses". The reconstruction_intent for
  shot N must describe the SAME subject and action that Pass A observed in
  shot N's own visual_description/subjects/actions.
- NEVER shift a shot's content to a neighboring shot ID. If shot 5 is a
  hand splashing water, its reconstruction_intent is about the hand
  splashing - even if shot 4 or shot 6 shows something more memorable.
- Do not rely on your own frame-by-frame reading of the video for per-shot
  content; the Pass A analyses are authoritative for what happens inside
  each deterministic shot.

## Reconstruction quality rules (v0.3)

- "global_brief" must be an actionable regeneration brief: subject, setting,
  mood, pacing, audio feel, and total duration target in one paragraph.
- EVERY entry in generation.shots MUST have a "reconstruction_intent" that a
  creator could shoot from directly, covering ALL of:
  (1) subject + specific action, (2) framing/camera position or movement,
  (3) approximate duration/pacing within the deterministic shot bounds,
  (4) any text, audio, or transition cue tied to that shot.
  One-line vague intents are not acceptable.

## Hard prohibitions

- Do NOT rewrite the deterministic media facts or shot boundaries. Use the
  shot IDs and timestamps exactly as provided; never propose new ones.
- Do NOT invent exact quotes or on-screen text that Pass A did not report.
- Do NOT invent specific audio/SFX identities.
- Ground every inference in cited evidence (shot IDs / timestamps / metadata).
